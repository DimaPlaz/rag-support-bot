# BoxHub Pricing and Unit Sizes

All prices are per month, in US dollars, and include VAT.

## Unit sizes and monthly prices

| Size | Area | Fits approximately | Riverside | Northgate |
|------|------|--------------------|-----------|-----------|
| XS (extra small) | 1 m² | 10–15 boxes, suitcases, seasonal items | $29 | $25 |
| S (small) | 3 m² | Contents of a studio room, a bicycle, sports gear | $59 | $49 |
| M (medium) | 6 m² | Contents of a one-bedroom apartment | $99 | $85 |
| L (large) | 10 m² | Contents of a two-bedroom apartment | $149 | $129 |
| XL (extra large) | 15 m² | Contents of a three-bedroom house, business stock | $199 | $175 |

Climate control at Northgate (S and M units only): +$15 per month.

## Discounts

- Prepay 6 months: 10% off the monthly price.
- Prepay 12 months: 15% off the monthly price.
- Students with a valid student card: 10% off any unit up to size M. Cannot be combined with prepayment discounts.
- First month for $1 on XS and S units for new customers who book online (promotion valid until further notice).

## Deposit

A refundable security deposit equal to one month's rent is required when you move in. It is returned within 7 business days after you move out and the unit passes inspection.
