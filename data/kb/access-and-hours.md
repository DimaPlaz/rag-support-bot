# Access and Opening Hours

## Facility access hours

You can access your unit every day from 6:00 to 23:00, including weekends and public holidays.

24/7 access is available at BoxHub Riverside for an extra $10 per month. Ask at the front desk to activate it.

## Office hours (front desk)

- Monday to Friday: 9:00 – 19:00
- Saturday: 10:00 – 16:00
- Sunday and public holidays: closed (facility access still works)

## How to get in

1. Enter your personal 6-digit access code at the gate keypad.
2. Use your access card to open the building door.
3. Open your unit with your own padlock.

Your access code is sent by SMS after you sign the agreement. Do not share it with people you have not authorized.

## Authorizing other people

You can authorize up to 3 additional people to access your unit. Add them through the front desk or by email. Each person gets their own access code. You are responsible for their actions at the facility.

## Lost access card or forgotten code

- Forgotten code: request a reset through the Telegram bot or by calling the front desk. The new code arrives by SMS within 10 minutes.
- Lost access card: report it immediately so we can block it. A replacement card costs $10.

## Locations

- **BoxHub Riverside**: 14 Harbor Lane, Riverside. Free parking for 10 cars, a loading dock, and freight elevators.
- **BoxHub Northgate**: 220 Industrial Road, Northgate. Drive-up units on the ground floor, parking for trucks up to 3.5 t.
