# Storage Rules

These rules keep everyone's belongings and our staff safe. Breaking them may lead to termination of the rental agreement.

## Allowed items

You can store household furniture, boxes, clothing, books, electronics, sports equipment, bicycles, documents and archives, business stock, and tools.

Motorcycles and scooters can be stored only in L and XL units at BoxHub Northgate, with an empty fuel tank and a disconnected battery.

## Prohibited items

The following items must never be stored at BoxHub:

- Flammable, explosive or toxic materials: gasoline, gas cylinders, paint thinners, fireworks, chemicals.
- Weapons and ammunition.
- Food and anything perishable, including pet food.
- Live animals or plants.
- Illegal goods, drugs, or stolen property.
- Items with strong odors or that could attract pests.
- Cash, jewelry, and irreplaceable valuables (these are not covered by protection).

## Using your unit

- You must not live, sleep or work in a unit.
- Do not connect electrical devices; units have no power outlets.
- Keep corridors clear. Do not leave items or trash outside your unit.
- Use only one padlock on your unit. You may use your own padlock or buy one at the front desk.
- Do not store items on top of the unit's mesh ceiling.

## Inspections

BoxHub may open a unit, by cutting the padlock if necessary, only in these cases: an emergency (fire, flood, police request), a suspected violation of these rules, or an unpaid balance over 60 days (see Payments and Late Fees). We notify you by phone and email before opening the unit, except in emergencies.
