# Payments and Late Fees

## How to pay

Rent is charged monthly in advance on your billing date, which is the day of the month you moved in.

Accepted payment methods:
- Automatic card payment (recommended): the card on file is charged on your billing date.
- Bank transfer (business customers only): pay to the account on your invoice, quoting your agreement number.
- Card or cash at the front desk during office hours.

Receipts and invoices are sent by email after each payment.

## Changing your payment card

Update your card through the Telegram bot or at the front desk. Changes take effect from the next billing date.

## Late payments

If a payment fails or is not made on the billing date:

| Days overdue | What happens |
|--------------|--------------|
| 1–5 days | Grace period. We send a reminder by SMS and email. No fee. |
| 6 days | A late fee of $20 is added. |
| 15 days | Your access code is suspended until the balance is paid. A further $20 fee is added. |
| 30 days | A formal written notice is sent. |
| 60 days | BoxHub may open the unit and move or sell the contents to cover the debt, as described in the Rental Agreement. |

Access is restored within one hour after the full balance is paid.

## Refunds

- Prepaid months that you do not use are refunded when you move out, minus any discount you received for prepaying.
- The security deposit is refunded within 7 business days after move-out inspection, minus any unpaid balance or cleaning fees.
- The monthly rent for the current month is not refunded if you leave mid-month.
