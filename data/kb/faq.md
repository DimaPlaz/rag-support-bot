# BoxHub Self-Storage — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is BoxHub?
BoxHub Self-Storage rents secure, clean storage units for personal and business use. We have two facilities: BoxHub Riverside and BoxHub Northgate. You can rent a unit for as little as one month.

### Do I need to sign a long-term contract?
No. The minimum rental period is one month. After that, your rental continues month to month until you give notice. Discounts are available if you prepay for 6 or 12 months (see Pricing).

### How do I rent a unit?
You can book online on our website, in the Telegram bot, or at the front desk of any facility. You will need a valid photo ID, a phone number, and a payment card. Online bookings are confirmed within 15 minutes during office hours.

### Can I see a unit before renting?
Yes. Visit any facility during office hours and a staff member will show you available units. No appointment is needed.

## Units

### What sizes are available?
We offer five sizes: XS (1 m²), S (3 m²), M (6 m²), L (10 m²) and XL (15 m²). See the Pricing document for prices and examples of what fits in each size.

### Are units climate-controlled?
All units at BoxHub Riverside are climate-controlled, with temperature kept between 10 °C and 25 °C and humidity below 60%. At BoxHub Northgate, climate control is available for S and M units for an extra fee.

### Can I change to a bigger or smaller unit?
Yes, at any time, subject to availability. Moving between units at the same facility is free; we even lend you a trolley. The new price applies from the next billing date.

## Moving in and out

### Do you provide moving help?
We lend trolleys and hand carts free of charge. We also sell boxes, tape and padlocks at the front desk. Professional movers are not included, but we can recommend partners.

### How do I end my rental?
Give at least 14 days' notice in writing (email, Telegram bot, or at the front desk). Empty and sweep the unit, remove your padlock, and return any access cards. Your deposit is refunded within 7 business days after inspection.

## Security

### How secure is my unit?
Every facility has 24/7 CCTV, an alarm system, and gated entry with personal access codes. Only you (and the people you authorize) can open your unit with your own padlock. Staff never have a key to your padlock.

### Is my stuff insured?
Basic protection covering up to $2,000 in value is included in every rental. You can buy extended protection up to $10,000 for $9 per month. Details are in the Rental Agreement.
